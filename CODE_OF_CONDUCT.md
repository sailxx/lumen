# Code of conduct

A short one, in the spirit of the big ones:

- Be kind and be precise — good bug reports are a gift.
- Assume good faith; everyone here is tinkering with their monitors' brightness, not saving lives.
- No harassment, personal attacks or discriminatory language. Keep it out of issues, reviews and code comments alike.
- Disagree with code, not with people.
- Off-topic or abusive content may be edited or removed at the maintainer's discretion.

Questions or reports: open an issue or use **Security → Report a vulnerability** for sensitive matters.
